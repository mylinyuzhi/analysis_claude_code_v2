# Changelog scoping + anchor probe: 2.1.215-2.1.220

Provenance document for the tail of the 2.1.193 → 2.1.220 window: **122 bullets across six releases**
(`.215`=1, `.216`=40, `.217`=20, `.218`=36, `.219`=24, `.220`=1). The shape is lop-sided: `.215` and `.220`
are single-line releases, `.216` and `.218` are giant bug-sweep releases (76 bullets between them, mostly
BUGFIX), and `.219` is the **Opus 5 launch release** — the only one in this sub-range with a large block of
genuinely new surface (`claude-opus-5`, `sandbox.network.strictAllowlist`, the `DirectoryAdded` hook,
`mcp_server_errors` in the headless init event, `workflowSizeGuideline`, nested-subagent stream-json
forwarding). `.217` is the *subagent-governor* release: it introduces both
`CLAUDE_CODE_MAX_CONCURRENT_SUBAGENTS` (default 20) and `CLAUDE_CODE_MAX_SUBAGENT_SPAWN_DEPTH`, which `.219`
then re-tunes (nesting default 1 → 3). Two threads run across the whole sub-range and are the richest
deep-dive targets: **screen-reader / accessibility plumbing** (`--ax-screen-reader`, announcement queue,
`CLAUDE_AX_STARTUP_QUIET_MS`) and **subagent resource governance** (depth, concurrency, `--max-budget-usd`,
worktree escape blocking). Three of the loudest-sounding bullets are narrower than they read: the sandbox
`strictAllowlist` and `filesystem.disabled` *enforcement* already existed in 2.1.193 (only the settings-file
surface is new), and "Removed Opus 4.7 from fast mode" is **not** in the shipped client eligibility list.

Bundles: `T = .../2.1.220/extract/cli_inner_pretty.js` (872,596 lines),
`B = .../2.1.193/extract/cli_inner_pretty.js` (718,679 lines). Every `Best 220 line` below is a line whose
text I read in the 2.1.220 bundle.
